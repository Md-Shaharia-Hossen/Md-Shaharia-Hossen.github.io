Who I Am

I am Md Shaharia Hossen, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from United International University. I am currently working as a Student Researcher at ELITE Lab, where my work spans artificial intelligence, computational biology, computer vision, and graph-based learning.


My long-term goal is to work as an AI and robotics engineer, developing intelligent autonomous systems that connect perception, learning, planning, and real-world robotic interaction.

Research Philosophy

I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary research where machine learning can solve real-world problems. My experience ranges from autonomous rover systems and ROS to deep learning, multiomics, spatial transcriptomics, computer vision, and multimodal AI.


I am currently looking for research opportunities, RA positions, and fully funded MS/PhD opportunities in robotics, AI, multi-robot systems, autonomous systems, optimization, and related areas.
